    Why players can audit results

    Keeping the balance airborne

    • Set a session limit before the first round and stop once it is reached, regardless of what the history shows.
    • Keep each stake near one or two percent of your bankroll so a bad run does not end the session too early.
    • Choose your exit target before take-off; deciding mid-climb is where discipline often fails.
    • Avoid increasing stakes after a loss, because the game has no memory and no obligation to correct itself.
    • Take breaks on a timer. Rounds are short, and repeated play can make small losses add up fast.
